## 1. Executive Summary

**NGO Reality (ngoreality.com)** is a trust and implementation platform for New Zealand nonprofits. It addresses a critical gap: thousands of NZ's ~29,000 registered charities have no online presence or outdated websites, which erodes public trust and hampers donations.

Using live Charities Register data and AI-powered tools, NGO Reality enables organisations to build a credible digital footprint through three revenue streams:

## 2. Problem Statements

1. **Invisible Nonprofits:** Roughly half of NZ's ~29,000 registered charities lack a usable website or clear online information. Thousands are effectively invisible to potential donors and volunteers.
2. **No Unified Trust Metric:** There is no standard certification or readily understandable trust symbol for charities. Donors cannot easily verify legitimacy or transparency.
3. **High Setup Costs:** Many small charities cannot afford professional web development or CRM systems. They remain stuck with outdated static pages or social media only.
4. **Technical Barriers:** Nonprofits often have little tech expertise. Setting up even a basic site or managing donor data takes a backseat to programme delivery.
5. **Missed Recognition:** Organisations that invest in quality websites or transparency have no mechanism to showcase this effort publicly.
6. **Lack of Ongoing Support:** Once a site exists, there is no system ensuring it stays updated. Downtime or outdated content goes unnoticed, damaging trust.

## 3. Solution Overview (Services & Tools)

NGO Reality offers an integrated platform combining **implementation**, **education**, and **verification**:

**Reality Badge + Membership ($100/year)**
Once a charity meets public trust standards (functional site, clear info, contact details), we award a verified badge with unique ID. Members get ~daily website monitoring, quarterly summary reports, and email alerts if their site goes down. Phone consultation included.

**Landing + Standards Package ($650)**
For charities needing a proper web presence — a professionally designed trust landing page plus standards setup, privacy policy templates, and education on best practices. Not the core revenue driver, but a high-value onboarding path.

**Organisation Workspace SaaS ($25/mo admin + $15/user)**
Online dashboard where charities track progress on a digital standards checklist, upload documents (financial reports, privacy policy), and manage their badge readiness. Separate from the internal staff CRM. ~25% of badge holders subscribe.

**AI-Powered Self-Service Tools**
Charities log in and create their own landing pages using AI assistance — auto-generated content templates, drag-and-drop design, and a simple CRM for contacts and donations.

**Custom Solutions**
Quoted projects for larger NGOs needing bespoke integrations, site builds, or remediation beyond the standard package.

## 5. Operations & Processes

- **Registry Pipeline:** ~100 leads/day imported from Charities Register (~1,000/month). Automated filtering for missing or non-functional websites. Each lead logged in internal CRM.
- **Outreach Automation:** Personalised email templates triggered by registry data. AI-assisted content generation. Founder handles follow-up calls and relationship building.
- **Onboarding:** Digital checklist sent on signup: site live, mission clear, contact form, privacy policy, donation info. Best-practice guides provided.
- **AI Platform:** AI assistant guides NGOs through content input. System generates draft landing pages for review and approval.
- **Verification Workflow:** Automated and manual checklist review. Once criteria are met, badge is issued with unique ID.
- **Monitoring:** ~Daily uptime checks via Go backend worker. Alerts trigger email notifications (Resend). Support via email and phone.
- **Internal Systems:** Private operations console (CRM) for tasks, subscriptions, pipeline, and finances. Clients use the Organisation Workspace (separate interface).

This lean, AI-powered workflow allows the founder to manage 1,000+ leads/month and 200+ badge conversions/month by year-end without staff.

## 10. Compliance & Risk Management

### Business Structure
NZ limited liability company. Companies Office, IRD obligations (GST registration when turnover exceeds $60K), annual returns, director duties.

### Legal & Regulatory
- **Charities Act:** Independent of charities registration — we do not function as a regulator.
- **Privacy/Data:** NZ Privacy Act compliance. No charity data shared without consent.
- **GST/Tax:** GST on applicable services once registered. Income tax provisioned monthly ($300–$600/mo in reserves). ACC levy provisioned at $90/mo.

### Insurance
Professional indemnity insurance before offering paid packages. General liability as needed.

### Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Slow early adoption | 1,000-lead/month pipeline keeps funnel full. $100/yr is low friction. |
| Long NGO sales cycles | Education-first $650 package builds trust. Evidence-based outreach. |
| Founder dependence | AI automation reduces manual work. Processes documented. Year-2 hire planned. |
| Technical failures | Redundant hosting, automated backups, Go-based monitoring worker. |
| Conversion targets missed | No staff costs = very low breakeven (~$6K/mo). Business stays profitable even at 50% of targets. |

### SWOT Summary

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|-----------|
| Zero staff costs, high margin | Single founder — capacity ceiling |
| Registry data = free lead pipeline | New market entrant |
| AI-powered automation | No brand recognition yet |
| Unique product (badge + monitoring + workspace) | |

| Opportunities | Threats |
